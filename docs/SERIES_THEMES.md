## The idea

When a reader enters a dungeon from a series they know, the backdrop, a few props and the System AI's lines should remind them of the book. Everything is **look and words only**: monsters, difficulty and rewards never change. Series not on this list keep the nine genre looks (Stone Halls, Mountain Sect and the rest).

**Which series.** These are the 41 series (3+ books) that at least one of the four readers has fully finished (library data, 2026-09-29).
- **Round 1** covers the 10 series shared by 2 or more readers, so the most people recognize them.
- **Round 2** covers the rest.

## What each series look can change

1. **Backdrop:** one of the existing nine, or a new base scene where needed (listed per entry).
2. **Props:** 2 to 4 small drawings layered on the backdrop, such as a sign, an object or a silhouette.
3. **Opening line:** what the System AI says on floor 1.
4. **Two or three extra System AI quips,** mixed in with the usual ones.
5. **Boss nickname (optional):** only the name changes, for example "The Ossuary King" becomes "The Ossuary King, Challenge Dungeon Guardian". Stats stay the same.

All lines are written fresh in the System AI's voice. Nothing is quoted from the books.

## Round 1: shared series (2 or more readers)

### He Who Fights with Monsters: "The Astral Oasis"
- **Backdrop:** a desert oasis town at night under a starry, cosmic sky, with a glowing green spring.
- **Props:** hedge-maze walls at the edges (where Jason woke up); a star-speckled cloak draped over a rock; a shadowy figure watching from the dark; a few floating lights drifting at the back.
- **Opening line:** "An astral space has opened. Everyone is very calm about this. Too calm."
- **Quips:** jokes about monologuing villains and people who won't stop talking mid-fight; the System AI complaining that you're being "a bit much".
- **Boss nickname:** "…, Astral King (Pretend)".

### Dungeon Crawler Carl: "The World Dungeon"
- **Backdrop:** Stone Halls turned into a game-show set: neon trim and a stairwell door.
- **Props:** a green-lit **Safe Room** sign; a **Desperado Club** neon sign; a TV screen with a climbing viewer count; a small jeweled tiara on a velvet pillow.
- **Opening line:** "Welcome, Crawler! The audience is watching. Please die in an entertaining way."
- **Quips:** viewer counts, sponsor boxes and the AI being delighted by your suffering. Our System AI already sounds like this, so it fits naturally.
- **Boss nickname:** "…, Floor Boss (Sponsored)".

### The Primal Hunter: "The Tutorial Forest"
- **Backdrop:** deep forest (new base) with huge trees.
- **Props:** a stone gate carved like a coiled serpent (the Viper's challenge dungeon); an alchemy table with bubbling green vials; a bow leaning on a tree.
- **Opening line:** "Somewhere, a very old snake is laughing at your stats."
- **Quips:** poison, alchemy, and "your Bloodline is tingling."
- **Boss nickname:** "…, Challenge Dungeon Guardian".

### System Universe: "The Integration Zone"
- **Backdrop:** Ruined City, with an alien invader ship hanging in the sky.
- **Props:** an innocent-looking bunny with red eyes; oversized butterflies; a spray-painted "SURVIVORS →" sign.
- **Opening line:** "Cute bunny detected. Do not pet the bunny."
- **Quips:** the invaders' paperwork; fight, survive, adapt.

### The Infinite World: "The Trial"
- **Backdrop:** Haunted Crypt restyled as a barren land of the dead, with a bone throne in the distance.
- **Props:** a Guard banner; a glowing Trial archway; a weathered summoning circle.
- **Opening line:** "Another Trial. The Guard sends its regards, and nothing else."

### The Perfect Run: "New Rome"
- **Backdrop:** Night City in Italian style, with a Colosseum silhouette covered in corporate billboards.
- **Props:** a glowing **SAVE POINT** marker (it could appear whenever the game saves your progress); a mafia car; a vending machine full of suspicious potions.
- **Opening line:** "Checkpoint reached. You may not reload. Yes, we checked."
- **Quips:** timelines, redo jokes, the System AI insisting it remembers the last loop.

### The Ripple System: "Earth Blood Online"
- **Backdrop:** Glitched Server with a fantasy overlay, like a game world with a few seams.
- **Props:** a gleaming talking axe on a pedestal (Frank); a "HEAD START: 3 DAYS" banner; a mansion AI butler hologram.
- **Opening line:** "Head start active. You bought every pass, so this dungeon is all yours. Congratulations. It's lonely."
- **Quips:** a smug, handsome axe; ripples and consequences.

### Tower of Jack: "The Tower"
- **Backdrop:** climbing tower (new base) with floors stacked into the sky.
- **Props:** a cracked moon over a city skyline (Broken Moon City); Black Centipede cult banners; a "DUNGEON TIMEOUT" sign.
- **Opening line:** "Dungeon timeout, again. The gods would like a word about your behavior."

### Art of the Adept: "Wurthaven"
- **Backdrop:** Arcane Academy, with enchanting tables and glowing rune circles instead of carpet.
- **Props:** a sorcerer's crest; a spell-book stand; a caged spirit orb.
- **Opening line:** "Wizards are considered support staff here. The dungeon disagrees."

### Azarinth Healer: "The Wilds of Elos"
- **Backdrop:** deep forest (new base) with grey ash drifting down.
- **Props:** a frontier town wall (Riverwatch); ash-dark trees; a green-glowing fist emblem carved in stone.
- **Opening line:** "Punch it. Heal. Punch it again. The Battle Healer method is very simple."

## Decisions for the owner

1. **Boss nicknames:** yes or no? They only change the name, never the stats.
2. **Character nods:** is it OK to reference characters by object, like the tiara on a pillow or Frank the axe? I've avoided drawing the actual characters so it stays tasteful and recognizable.
3. **Order:** Round 1 first (10 series, reaching 2 to 3 readers each), then Round 2 in whatever order the family cares about most?
4. **Anything wrong or missing?** You know these books better than any summary. Swap in anything the family would recognize instantly.
