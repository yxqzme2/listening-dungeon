# Changelog

## 1.1 (first public release)

- **The Arena:** fight another player's ghost (their real stats and gear), climb the ladder, weekly prizes for #1 and the most wins, monthly Arena Champion.
- **Daily shared Store:** restocks at midnight, first come first served, a rare Legendary about once a year.
- **Best gear** button and class-aware upgrade markers in the bag and Store.
- **Series complete:** 5 bookmarks per book, with a popup; 5+ book series still guarantee an Epic.
- Monster attack scales with your attack (health gear no longer backfires); no one-hit kills on elites and bosses.
- Party page shows everyone's stats; tap a friend's gear to compare it with yours.
- Title screen: crawler levels and a Library button.

## 1.0

- The Listening Dungeon: books become dungeons, seven classes, gear and the Forge, badges, the December party boss, Tier Lists, Recommendations, Chronicle, Release Radar, Requests, the Monster Maker.
